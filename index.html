Legal FAQs

What is an FIR?
A First Information Report is a written document prepared by the police when they receive information about a cognizable offence.
What is an Affidavit?
An affidavit is a written statement of facts confirmed by the person making it by oath before an authorised officer.
What is a Legal Notice?
A legal notice is a formal communication sent to a person informing them of an intention to take legal action.
